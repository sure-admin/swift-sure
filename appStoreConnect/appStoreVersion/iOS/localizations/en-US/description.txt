Sure Insights is the native (read-only for now!) companion for your Sure Finances personal finance server installation.  It also helps you visualize your Apple Card and Cash on-device expenses for free.

Connect securely to your server to sync FinanceKit transactions and review your net worth, accounts, budgets, and proactive financial insights. Continue conversations with Sure Assistant wherever you are, and optionally receive notifications when new insights are ready.

Your credentials are stored securely in the device Keychain. Sure Insights connects directly to the Sure server you choose.

Usage analytics
To improve the app, Sure Insights sends app-open and screen-view events, an anonymous installation identifier, and app and device information to PostHog in the United States. PostHog also captures your IP address and uses it to derive approximate location for analytics. Analytics is enabled by default. You can turn it off at any time under Sure connection → Usage analytics → Share usage analytics. Financial data, credentials, server addresses, and conversation content are not included in these analytics events. Session recording is disabled.
